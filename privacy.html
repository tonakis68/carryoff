Privacy Policy

Version 28 November 2026 · Bulgarian version below

1. Who we are

CarryOff (carryoff.eu) is operated by TONAKIS Ltd., company ID (ЕИК) 208759663, registered office: zh.k. Chayka, bl. 28, entr. G, floor 8, apt. 82, Varna, Bulgaria (“we”, “us”). We decide how and why your personal data is used (we are the “controller”). Contact: office@carryoff.eu.

2. What data we collect

If you book storage: your name, email address and phone number; the place, date, times and bags you booked; the price and payment status; the answer you give to “How did you hear about us?”; and a photo of your bags taken by the place when it receives them. If you cancel or are refunded, we keep a record of that.

If you apply to be or are a storage partner: business name and company ID, registered and shop address, the contact person and legal representative, phone, email, opening hours, bank account (IBAN) for payouts, and your booking and earnings records.

Technical data: when you use the website, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic device data to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics. Your browser stores your language choice, and partners who log in receive a login cookie that is needed for the login to work.

We never see or store your card number. Payment is handled by Stripe.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide your booking, send you the confirmation, QR code and status emails, process cancellations and refunds, and answer your questions (needed to perform our contract with you).
  • To protect your bags: the photo taken at drop-off records their condition (our legitimate interest in handling claims, and yours in being protected).
  • To pay partners and keep accounting and tax records (a legal obligation).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud and abuse, and to understand how customers find us (our legitimate interests). You can object, see section 7.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decisions about you.

4. Who receives your data

  • The storage place you book sees only your first name, your luggage and the times. Your address, email and phone number are not shown to it.
  • Stripe processes your payment and refunds.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Supabase hosts our database (in the EU).
  • Cloudflare hosts and protects our website.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map images, so your browser contacts its servers when the map is shown.
  • Authorities, accountants and legal advisers, where the law requires it or we need them to handle a claim.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area. Where your data is transferred there, we rely on the safeguards approved under EU law, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. How long we keep it

  • The photo of your bags is deleted when your bags are handed back, or refused.
  • Booking and payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, and as needed to deal with claims.
  • One-time login codes expire within minutes.
  • Other data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or made anonymous.

6. Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Access to the database is restricted to our server and to us. Partners can see only their own bookings, and only the details needed to take in and hand back bags.

7. Your rights

You have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to office@carryoff.eu with your booking code and we will answer within one month. Some records we must keep by law, and we will tell you if that applies. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД, cpdp.bg) or to the data protection authority of your own country.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the current version is at the top of the page.
